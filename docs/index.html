Your PC, in your hand.

A tiny island on your Windows desktop. A landscape remote on your iPhone. Apps in between.

On the PC

The island

An OLED-black pill that floats over everything, snaps to any edge, and expands when you need it.

On the iPhone

The remote

A native app in landscape. Pair once with a six-digit code, then it just reconnects.
