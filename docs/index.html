Your PC, for your phone.

A small island on your Windows desktop, and a landscape remote on your phone.

01 / Desktop

On the PC

The island stays on top of your windows, snaps to any edge of any screen, and expands when you hover it.

02 / Phone

On the phone

A native app made for landscape. Pair it once with a six-digit code and it reconnects by itself.

03 / Apps

Apps

Music, Discord, the mixer and the rest are apps you install from the island. They update on their own.

Catalog

Apps

Install them from the island: Settings → Apps.

An app is a folder with some JavaScript and a JSON screen. Make your own.
