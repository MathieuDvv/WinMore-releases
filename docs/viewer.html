    A place to experiment

    Find your layout.
    Then make it native.

    This viewer interprets Kit with its own quiet, monochrome style. Test spacing, arrangement, bindings, conditional content and local interactions across screen sizes.

    For lists and data-driven screens, add representative values in Mock data. The viewer never runs your app's JavaScript. Web blocks, video and native-only actions have placeholders or activity entries. Remote images stay blocked; packaged images are available from folders and wmapp files.

    It is a layout playground, not an exact iPhone or Android simulation. Check your final app on both phones.
