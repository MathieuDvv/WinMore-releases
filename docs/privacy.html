Onbun privacy policy

Updated 14 November 2026 · Mathieu Da Vinha

Who is responsible

Onbun is developed and provided by Mathieu Da Vinha. This policy covers the Onbun iPhone and iPad app and its desktop companion, including Onbun for macOS. For privacy questions or requests, email mathieu.davinha@icloud.com.

Onbun turns your phone into a control surface for apps running on a computer you own. You choose which computer to pair with, which desktop apps to install and which services to use. Those services and desktop apps may have additional privacy practices.

Information processed on your devices

To discover and pair devices, Onbun processes local network addresses, computer and device names, a computer identifier and pairing credentials. It stores the connection so you can reconnect. On iPhone and iPad, the pairing credential is stored in device-only Keychain storage and is not synced through iCloud Keychain. Navigation choices and screen preferences are stored locally.

The desktop companion processes information needed by the apps you enable. Depending on those apps, this can include playback information, messages, browser screenshots, clipboard contents, files, application names, agent activity and text you type. It sends the data needed for the screen you open to your paired phone and receives the actions you choose. Desktop apps run with the companion’s access to your computer; install only apps you trust.

When you choose a photo in the iOS system picker, Onbun receives that selected photo and can save a resized copy in its local storage. It does not request unrestricted access to your photo library. An explicit paste action can read your phone’s clipboard, subject to iOS permissions. A desktop clipboard app can read your computer’s clipboard while you use that feature.

The desktop companion stores app settings, installed packages, service sessions and app data in its local profile. Websites opened on the computer may store cookies and sign-in data there. Onbun does not create a separate Onbun cloud account for you.

Network connections and security

Onbun 0.9.1 and later encrypts and authenticates commands, screen data and package files exchanged through the paired phone–computer link. Both apps must support that version. Nearby-device discovery and initial setup still expose basic connection metadata, such as local addresses and device names, to the local network. The pairing code is not sent as plain text.

A pairing link contains a credential that allows control of the computer. Keep it private and do not put it in public reports. Encryption does not protect a compromised device, an untrusted desktop app or someone who already has your pairing credential. Use Forget phones on the computer to replace its pairing key and revoke existing pairings.

Third-party services and sharing

Onbun does not send your pairing credential, commands or screen content to an Onbun-operated server. The iOS app contains no advertising or analytics service, and Onbun does not sell your personal information or use it for advertising tracking.

When you use media, web screens or service integrations, your phone or computer can connect directly to third-party providers. Those providers receive information needed for the request, such as your IP address, requested content and any account data or text you choose to submit. Their own privacy policies and account controls apply. This includes any AI service you choose through a desktop app; Onbun does not independently send your content to an AI provider for its own purposes.

A package web screen receives its own app’s screen data and can issue supported commands to that app on your paired computer. Public HTTPS pages loaded as web screens do not receive the Onbun native bridge or its screen data. Do not assume an installed desktop app is isolated from other computer data.

The desktop companion contacts GitHub for its app catalog, package downloads and desktop updates. GitHub and other download hosts process network requests under their policies. iOS app updates are delivered by Apple through TestFlight or the App Store. Apple handles distribution, TestFlight diagnostics and feedback under its own policies. No third-party provider is entitled by this policy to use Onbun-held data for unrelated purposes.

Permissions and your choices

Local Network access allows the iOS app to find and connect to your computer. You can change that permission in iOS Settings; connection features will be unavailable while access is denied. Photo selection and clipboard access use the system’s picker and permission controls. You can cancel those actions and continue using other features.

You control service sign-ins and installed desktop apps on your computer. Sign out of a service or remove an app you no longer want to use. Onbun’s settings let you change the phone’s navigation without creating an online account.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences, selected photo copies, service sessions and caches remain on the relevant device until cleared, replaced or removed. Storage and backup behavior also depends on that device’s operating system. The device-only iOS pairing credential is excluded from migration to other devices.

On iPhone or iPad, Settings → Forget this PC removes the saved local connection. Settings → Erase local data also removes saved screen preferences, navigation choices, selected photo copies and the image cache. Web screens use nonpersistent website storage. These controls do not delete your original photos, computer files or third-party accounts. Uninstalling alone may leave a Keychain credential behind; use Forget this PC or Erase local data first.

On the computer, Forget phones revokes existing pairings. Disconnect services and remove their local app data or the companion profile to clear desktop-held information. The packaged macOS app keeps its profile in ~/Library/Application Support/WinMore; quit Onbun before removing that folder, because doing so removes installed apps, saved settings and sessions. Ask for help if you need to retain part of that data.

If you contact the developer, the information you send is used to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Support correspondence is retained while needed for that purpose and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Email the privacy contact to request access, correction or deletion of information you supplied directly. Data stored only on your devices cannot be retrieved or deleted remotely by the developer. Third-party account requests must be made to the relevant provider.

Children, changes and contact

Onbun is a general-purpose computer companion and is not designed specifically for children. The developer does not knowingly request children’s personal information. Third-party content and services may have their own age restrictions. If you believe a child has supplied personal information directly to the developer, contact the email address below.

This policy may change as Onbun’s features or data practices change. The updated date identifies the current version. Material changes will be described with the relevant app update or notice. For privacy questions, deletion requests or support, contact mathieu.davinha@icloud.com. Avoid sending passwords, pairing links or private screen content unless it is necessary to explain your issue, and redact sensitive details.
